    The bite rule

    The whole market runs on one comparison, applied square inch by square inch.

    Someone holds the space

    Every claim carries a density: what they paid, divided by the area they got. That number is the wall around it.

    density = offer ÷ area

    A richer offer overlaps it

    The overlap is decided per square inch, not per deal. $26 beats $18, so those inches change hands the moment the claim lands.

    if new > held → inches move

    The shape gets a bite in it

    Velour keeps the L that's left and is refunded for what it lost. Rectangles are only ever a starting position.

    refund = density × inches lost
