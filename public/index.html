How it works

Two sides, one garment

If you are a sponsor

You are buying a position, not a post

  1. Browse the garments and open the one whose room you want to be in.
  2. Drag out the patch of fabric you want, front or back. The price follows the size as you drag.
  3. Bid your maximum. We bid the least you need to hold it and never more than your number.
  4. Upload your mark and pay by card. The wearer says yes, and it is printed for the day.

If you are the one wearing it

Your garment is the only line that can earn

  1. Photograph yourself front and back in what you are going to wear. Both are required.
  2. Set one rate — what 1% of the garment costs — and how much more the front is worth.
  3. Sponsors draw the patch they want. You see exactly where, and accept or decline each one.
  4. Open the bidding. You are paid the settled price in full — the sponsor pays our fee on top.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What if the spot I want is already taken?
Take it off them. Every spot stays contestable until it closes: bid more than the standing price plus 15% and it is yours. The sponsor that held it has its hold released in full, automatically. Nothing is locked until the clock runs out.
How does bidding your maximum work?
You state the most you are willing to pay. The spot then sits at the least you need to hold it — not at your maximum. If someone bids under your ceiling, your price rises just enough to stay ahead and you keep the spot. If someone goes over it, you are outbid and your hold is released. You are never charged more than the number you typed, and bidding your true ceiling early costs you nothing.
When am I actually charged?
Your card is authorised when you bid, which places a hold rather than a charge. If you are outbid, the hold is released and nothing is ever taken. If you are still holding the spot when it closes, the hold is captured and you are charged the settled price — which is often less than your maximum. Your bank may take a few days to drop the pending line even though we release it immediately. That is the issuer, not a charge.
What if the goal is not reached?
Every hold is released in full. The goal is the number the wearer needs for the whole thing to be worth printing; below it, the campaign does not run and nobody is charged.
Can a bid be sniped in the last second?
No. Any bid inside the final ten minutes pushes that spot's close out by ten minutes. A spot only closes once nobody has bid for ten clear minutes, so the last bid is always one somebody had a chance to answer.
What format does my logo need to be?
SVG or a PNG on transparency, at least 1000px on the long edge. Solid single-colour marks print best on fabric. Upload it when you bid and it appears on the garment straight away.
